Meme Mimic support

Quick fixes for the most common problems.

The game can't hear me

  • Allow the microphone for this site. On a computer, click the microphone or lock icon in the address bar. On iPhone, tap aA in Safari's address bar → Website Settings → Microphone → Allow.
  • Close other apps or tabs that are using the microphone, like video calls.
  • Hold the device closer and copy the sound clearly. A take that is too quiet isn't scored, and you can simply go again.
  • No microphone? Use Demo take on any turn. Demo scores are clearly labeled.

I can't hear the sounds

  • Turn up the volume and tap Play the sound again. Browsers only start audio after you tap something on the page.
  • The speaker button at the top mutes sound effects only. The meme sounds always play.
  • On iPhone, check that the phone isn't connected to a Bluetooth speaker or headphones you don't expect.

The 3D characters don't appear

Meme Mimic uses WebGL for its 3D stage. Update your browser, or turn on hardware acceleration in its settings. If WebGL isn't available, the game still works with character portraits.

My crew disappeared

Your crew is saved in your browser on this device. Private browsing, clearing site data or switching browsers starts a fresh crew.

Scoring

Your take is compared with the original on pitch shape (55%), rhythm (30%) and length (15%). Pitch is measured relative to each sound's own middle note, so you don't need to hit the same notes, just the same ups and downs.

iPhone and iPad app

For help with the app's gameplay, the microphone, or privacy, contact us through the App Store page. Please include your app version and iOS version.

Privacy

Recordings are scored on your device and never uploaded. Read the full privacy policy.
