# Echo Bloom: 3D Character Brief

**App:** Echo Bloom is a party game for iPhone (1–8 players). Each round the phone plays a funny sound, players take turns copying it into the mic, and the app scores each copy out of 100. Every player is represented by a 3D mascot on a stage, and the mascot reacts live: it listens, sings while you record, then celebrates or sulks at your score.

**Need:** an original, lovable mascot character for this game. Design it freshly; you choose the creature, shape, and personality. It should:
- Feel fun, musical, and party-like
- Read clearly at small sizes on a phone screen
- Have a very expressive face (it has to show listening, singing, joy, and disappointment)
- Work in many body colors, one per player
- Have a simple, clean shape that's easy to animate in 3D

**Style:** high-quality 3D, premium mobile-game look, appealing and polished. Not based on any existing character or brand.

**Deliver:**
1. 3 different character concepts side by side
2. A front/side T-pose sheet on a white background for the chosen one, for 3D modeling
