Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy covers Meme Mimic on the web at mememimic.com and the Meme Mimic app for iPhone and iPad. In both, your voice never leaves your device.

Web game · iPhone and iPad app

Meme Mimic on the web

Microphone

Your browser asks for microphone access the first time you record a take. The microphone turns on when you start a take, just before the 3‑2‑1 count-in, and is released as soon as the take stops. Only the audio after the count-in is used.

Recordings

Each take is analyzed in your browser to produce the score. It is kept in memory for the current turn only, so you can play it back on the score screen, and is discarded when the next turn starts. Recordings are never uploaded, sent to a server or saved to disk.

Data saved on your device

Player names, characters, colors, the number of rounds, your chosen sound packs and your sound and vibration settings are saved in your browser's local storage so your crew is ready next time. This data stays on your device. Clearing your browser's site data, or Settings → Reset crew, removes it.

Offline files

To load faster and work offline, the game stores its own files (code, characters and sounds) in your browser's cache. These are the game's files, not your data.

Third parties

The page loads its font from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details when the font is requested. The web game has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers.

Meme Mimic for iPhone and iPad

Microphone

Meme Mimic uses the microphone only while you record a turn. Your recording is analyzed on your device and is never uploaded or saved.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google Mobile Ads. Depending on your region and choices, Google may process device, advertising, and interaction information to provide and measure ads.

Tracking permission

The app may ask for permission to track. You can decline, or change your choice later in iOS Settings.

Consent choices

Where required, the app asks for consent before requesting ads. You can review these choices from the Privacy section in the app's Settings.

Data on your device

Player names and preferences are stored only on your device. No account is needed. Deleting the app removes this data.

Children

Meme Mimic doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change too.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See the support page.
